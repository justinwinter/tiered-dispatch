- [ ] **SOC 2 / ISO 27001 badge** — only if/when the org runs infrastructure

      Do not pre-claim.
- [ ] **Independent replication** — a third party re-running the eval harness
      and publishing congruent numbers. The single highest-value trust unlock;
      becomes a footer link ("Independently replicated by …").
